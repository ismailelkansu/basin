BASIN

Shape the land. Water does the rest.
Your move: tap a cell to DIG it lower or FILL it higher. That is the whole game — you never touch the water.
Water flows from your spring: downhill freely, across flat ground only a short way. It never climbs.
Land your water reaches is yours. Where both waters meet, nobody owns it.
Win: pour your water into the enemy spring — or own more land when the moves run out.
Numbers on cells show height. Dig channels to attack, raise walls to defend… but every wall blocks water on both sides.
